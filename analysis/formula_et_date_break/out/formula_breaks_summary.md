## How to read

- **Date breaks** come from `PAY_PayrollBackFormula` months; consecutive identical ET-slot signatures are collapsed into one `EffectiveFromMonth`.
- **ET breaks** come from args of `FxPAY_PersonnelKind` / `Kind2` / `PersonnelEmploymentType` / `KindSepecial` (see function bodies in source DB).
- In-SQL `CASE BFactor10_A WHEN 31…` day-count branches are **not** split; they remain inside `OutputExpr` / `OuterWrapper`.
- `F6` هیئت مدیره is a runtime flag (factor 1506), not a migrated employment type.
